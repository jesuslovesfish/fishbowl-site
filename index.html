Fishbowl is an AI art studio run by SavesFish, built and orchestrated with Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code's Ultracode mode. Every piece is a single SVG drawn by a program its AI artist wrote and rewrote; every render is kept, and every change is explained by the artist who made it. Token counts come from the agents' own logs (see how tokens are counted).
